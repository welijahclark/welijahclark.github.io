### Some of my projects:

#### [Linked File Project](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/william.elijah.clark/viz/Massachusetts_K-12_Data_Visualization/MassachusettsK-12EducationTableauDataVisualizationProject)
<img src="images/apples_and_oranges.png?raw=true"/>

This Tableau project explores K-12 education data from the Commonwealth of Massachusetts. The dashboards in this presentation explore questions such as "Which schools have higher drop-out rates versus non-traditional school completion paths?", "Which school districts have higher average SAT scores?", and "How do student ratios impact college attendance?". You can watch a video of me walking through it [here](https://lnkd.in/p/gcfU5N4J), or go through these dashboards for yourself [here](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/william.elijah.clark/viz/Massachusetts_K-12_Data_Visualization/MassachusettsK-12EducationTableauDataVisualizationProject)


#### [FSU Libraries Student Engagement Survey Project](https://repository.lib.fsu.edu/islandora/object/fsu:857160)
<img src="images/FSU_analysis_career_histogram.png?raw=true">

While undergraduate students often say that data skills are important for their careers, does this translate into data service usage? This research project aimed to gather insights on that question for Florida State University Libraries. You can read the full report [here](https://repository.lib.fsu.edu/islandora/object/fsu:857160), or take a look at my R code and analyses [here](https://github.com/welijahclark/FSU-Libraries-Data-Fellowship-Survey-Project/tree/main).
